# Acoustic guitar sample attribution

Acoustic guitar samples from [tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), maintained and edited by **Nicholaus P. Brosowsky (nbrosowsky)**. The repository credits **Iowa / University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples** as the original acoustic guitar sample source: <http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/>.

Credit and the sample license are documented in the pinned upstream [README](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5/README.md#license) and [sample-source-info.txt](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5/sample-source-info.txt). The repository's MIT license applies to code; its README separately licenses samples under CC BY 3.0.

Source revision: [`622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5`](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/commit/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5).
